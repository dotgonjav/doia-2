Founder & interior architect

Ethos

Inspired by nature and ritual.

Your space is designed around who you are. Nature provides the material and the light; your rituals, how you wake, cook, receive and come to rest, provide the form.

Whether it is your home, an office, a shop or a place for guests: you work directly with one interior architect. The result feels personal, is timeless and is made to last.

© Dulguun Otgonjav
Who for?

An interior that is entirely yours.

For those who want no generic interior and no choice between three moodboards. Your project begins with a real conversation: how you move through a space, where you linger, what you always wanted but never found the words for.

The result is an interior tuned to how the space is really used, not to how it photographs.

Material

Material you feel before you see it.

A material determines how a space feels every day, and what it still has to say twenty years from now. Oak, natural stone, clay and linen do not age, they mature, and are repaired instead of replaced.

Sustainability here is not an addition, but the result of choosing well.

Scent

Untreated wood and natural stone carry a subtle, living scent, a space that smells of what it is, not of lacquer or glue.

Temperature

Stone holds coolness in summer, wood carries warmth before you even touch it. Material that moves with the season.

Texture

The grain of oak, the tooth of clay, the hand of linen, surfaces the fingertips recognise and that invite touch.

What drives the work

Quality over quantity. Always.

Real attention cannot be scaled. That is why only a limited number of projects are taken on each year, so your project receives the full weight it deserves.
