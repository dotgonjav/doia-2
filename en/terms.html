1. Scope

1.1 These general terms and conditions apply to all quotations issued by the interior architect and to all assignments entrusted to them by a private client (a consumer). “The client” is the person or persons to whom the quotation is addressed. If there are two clients, each of them is liable for payment of the full fee.

1.2 The interior architect provides these general terms and conditions together with the quotation, so that the client can read them before committing.

1.3 Deviations from these general terms and conditions apply only if the parties agree to them in writing (an email is sufficient).

2. Documents and order of precedence

2.1 For an assignment under the “Design” or “Design with guidance” package, the parties enter into a written agreement. These general terms and conditions form part of that agreement.

2.2 If the documents conflict with one another, the following order applies:

  • the specific arrangements in the agreement;
  • the other provisions of the agreement;
  • the quotation;
  • these general terms and conditions.

2.3 If a provision is open to more than one interpretation, the interpretation most favourable to the client applies.

3. Quotation and formation of the assignment

3.1 A quotation is valid for 14 calendar days from its date, unless the quotation states a different period.

3.2 The quotation is based on the information provided by the client and, where applicable, on a site visit. If that information proves incorrect or incomplete, the parties may adjust the price and the time limits by mutual agreement.

3.3 For the “Design” and “Design with guidance” packages, the interior architect sends a draft agreement together with the quotation. The client accepts the quotation by signing that agreement. Only then is the assignment formed. Approval of the quotation without signing the agreement is not sufficient for this purpose.

3.4 For smaller assignments for which no agreement is drawn up (e.g. a consultation or limited advice on an hourly basis), the assignment is formed when the client accepts the quotation in writing (an email is sufficient). These general terms and conditions apply in full to such assignments.

3.5 An introductory meeting or site visit before acceptance of the quotation is free of charge, unless the interior architect has communicated a price in writing in advance and the client has accepted it.

4. Prices

4.1 All prices in quotations and agreements include 21% VAT. If the VAT rate changes before a service has been fully invoiced, the amount still to be invoiced is adjusted to the new rate.

4.2 Prices are fixed and are not indexed. They change only if the assignment is modified or extended and the client has accepted that change and its price in advance in writing (an email is sufficient), or in the cases expressly stated in the agreement.

4.3 Prices include all costs of the interior architect, unless the quotation or the agreement expressly states other costs (e.g. travel costs).

4.4 Additional work and smaller assignments on an hourly basis are charged at 84.70 euros per hour, including VAT, unless the quotation or the agreement states a different amount. The interior architect carries out additional work only after the client has agreed to it in advance in writing (an email is sufficient). Correcting errors or omissions in the interior architect’s work is never additional work.

5. Performance of the assignment

5.1 The interior architect performs the assignment with the care of a normally diligent interior architect. Their obligations are obligations of means (best-efforts obligations): they do not guarantee a specific result, unless expressly agreed otherwise.

5.2 The interior architect does not carry out any works and does not supply materials or furniture. The works are carried out by contractors with whom the client enters into a written agreement directly.

5.3 The interior architect is the client’s technical adviser. They are not the client’s authorised agent: they do not sign agreements, orders or official reports on behalf of the client, unless the client expressly instructs them to do so in writing.

5.4 The client provides the interior architect in good time with all information needed for the project, including their wishes and needs, the available plans, any permits, the co-ownership regulations and the necessary consents (e.g. from the association of co-owners or the landlord). The client is responsible for the accuracy of that information.

5.5 The estimates prepared by the interior architect are estimates based on average market prices and the characteristics of the project. They are not fixed prices. The actual cost depends on the contractors’ quotations, on changes requested by the client and on unforeseen circumstances during the works.

5.6 The time limits in the quotation or the agreement are extended if the delay results from late information or approval by the client, from changes requested by the client, from force majeure or from a suspension under article 6.4. The interior architect informs the client of this in writing as soon as possible.

5.7 If the interior architect, through their own fault, substantially exceeds a time limit, the client may set a reasonable new time limit in writing. If that time limit is not met either, article 9.3 applies.

6. Payment

6.1 Invoices are payable within 15 days of the invoice date, to the account number stated on the invoice.

6.2 If an advance payment has been agreed and the client has a right of withdrawal (article 7), the advance is due only after the withdrawal period has ended, or earlier if the client asked for work to start during that period.

6.3 If the client objects to an invoice, they notify this in writing (an email is sufficient), with an explanation, within 15 days of receipt. The undisputed part of the invoice remains due.

6.4 Late payment. If an invoice is not paid on time, the interior architect first sends a free reminder with a new payment period of 14 calendar days. That period starts on the third working day after a paper reminder is sent, or on the day after a reminder is sent by email. If the invoice is still unpaid after that period:

  • default interest is due at the rate set out in article 5, second paragraph, of the Belgian Act of 2 August 2002 on combating late payment in commercial transactions, from the end of that period;
  • a fixed compensation is due of at most: 20 euros if the outstanding amount does not exceed 150 euros; 30 euros plus 10% of the amount between 150.01 and 500 euros if the outstanding amount is between 150.01 and 500 euros; 65 euros plus 5% of the amount above 500 euros, up to a maximum of 2,000 euros, if the outstanding amount exceeds 500 euros (article XIX.4 of the Belgian Code of Economic Law);
  • the interior architect may, after written notice, suspend further work until the invoice is paid. The time limits of the assignment are then extended by the same duration.

6.5 If the interior architect owes the client an amount (for example a refund) and it is not paid on time, the client is entitled, after a reminder with a period of 14 calendar days, to the same interest and the same fixed compensation.

7. Right of withdrawal

7.1 If an agreement is concluded away from the interior architect’s business premises (e.g. at the client’s home) or at a distance (e.g. by email), the client has the right to withdraw from the agreement within 14 calendar days, counting from the day after its conclusion, without giving any reason and free of charge (articles VI.47 and VI.67 of the Belgian Code of Economic Law).

7.2 The client informs the interior architect of this clearly, by letter to the office address or by email to one of the interior architect’s email addresses stated in these general terms and conditions. The client may use the model form in the annex for this purpose, but is not obliged to do so. It is sufficient for the client to send their decision before the end of the period.

7.3 If the client wishes the interior architect to start the assignment during the withdrawal period, the client makes an express request to that effect on paper or by email. If the client then withdraws from the agreement, they pay an amount proportionate to what the interior architect has already provided. With that request, the client expressly acknowledges that they lose their right of withdrawal once the assignment has been fully performed.

7.4 If the client withdraws from the agreement, the interior architect refunds all amounts received within 14 days, less the amount referred to in article 7.3.

8. Liability and insurance

8.1 The ten-year liability under articles 1792 and 2270 of the former Belgian Civil Code (serious defects that endanger the stability of the building) remains fully applicable.

8.2 For all other faults, the interior architect bears only the damage caused by their own fault, in proportion to their share in that damage. They cannot be held liable for the whole (in solidum) together with the contractors or other specialists. This limitation does not apply in the event of intent or gross negligence on the part of the interior architect.

8.3 The interior architect is not liable for errors by the contractors in the execution of the works, or for errors in plans, studies or site supervision by third parties, unless those errors result (in part) from a fault in the interior architect’s own services.

8.4 As required by the Belgian Act of 9 May 2019, the interior architect is insured for professional liability. The name and company number of the insurer and the policy number are stated in the general terms and conditions the client receives together with the quotation, and in the agreement. A certificate of this insurance is provided to the client on simple request.

8.5 The interior architect points out to the client that they can take out additional insurance, for example against fire, water damage, storm, theft, neighbour nuisance (article 3.101 of the Belgian Civil Code) and construction site risks (a so-called contractors’ all-risk policy). For the exact cover and exclusions, the client should consult an insurer.

9. End of the assignment

9.1 Termination by the client. The client may terminate an assignment in writing at any time. For an assignment with an agreement, the rules of that agreement apply. For a smaller assignment without an agreement, the client pays only for the services already provided.

9.2 Death or incapacity for work. An assignment is given in view of the person of the parties. In the event of the death of a party, or the permanent incapacity for work of the interior architect, the assignment ends automatically. The fee for services already provided remains due.

9.3 Fault of the interior architect. If the interior architect fails to fulfil their obligations, the client may give them formal notice by registered letter, with a period of 15 days to remedy the situation. If this does not happen, the client may terminate the assignment with a second registered letter. The interior architect is then entitled only to the fee for services already provided and usable, and is not entitled to any compensation. The client retains their right to damages under the general rules.

9.4 Fault of the client. If the client fails to fulfil their obligations, e.g. by not paying invoices, the interior architect may give them formal notice by registered letter, with a period of 15 days to remedy the situation. If this does not happen, the interior architect may terminate the assignment with a second registered letter. The client then pays the fee for services already provided and, for an assignment with an agreement, the compensation provided for in that agreement.

10. Force majeure

10.1 Neither party is liable for a failure resulting from force majeure: an event beyond its control that it could not reasonably foresee or avoid and that makes the performance of its obligations impossible. The party invoking force majeure notifies the other party in writing as soon as possible.

10.2 The time limits are extended by the duration of the force majeure. If the force majeure makes further performance permanently impossible, the assignment ends and the client pays the fee for services already provided. Neither party then owes any compensation.

11. Copyright

11.1 The interior architect is the author of their designs. They retain all copyright in their plans, sketches, studies, images, execution files and in the executed design. They deliver their documents digitally as PDF; source files (such as CAD drawings and 3D models) are not transferred.

11.2 Once the client has paid the fee for the services concerned, they may have the design executed once in the property to which the assignment relates. They may provide the plans to third parties (such as contractors) only for the execution of that project. Services that have not been paid for may not be used.

11.3 If the project is executed without the interior architect, the client respects the design and contacts the interior architect before making any significant changes to it.

11.4 The interior architect may, in consultation with the client as to the timing, take photographs of the site and the executed design and use them for their portfolio and publications. The address and identity of the client are not disclosed without the client’s consent. The interior architect may discreetly display their name at the work.

12. Personal data

The interior architect uses the client’s personal data only for the performance of the assignment and to comply with legal obligations, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The client may always consult their data and have it corrected.

13. Complaints and disputes

13.1 The parties first make every effort to resolve a dispute by mutual agreement. They may call on an accredited mediator for this purpose. The client may also contact the Consumer Ombudsman Service (www.consumentenombudsdienst.be).

13.2 Belgian law applies to all quotations and assignments. If an amicable solution cannot be reached, the court with jurisdiction under the statutory rules is competent.

14. Final provisions

14.1 If a provision of these general terms and conditions is void or unenforceable, the other provisions remain in force.

14.2 The interior architect may amend these general terms and conditions. An amendment applies only to quotations sent after the amendment. For an ongoing assignment, the version the client received together with the quotation continues to apply.
