Ethos

Inspired by nature and ritual.

Your space is designed around who you are. Nature provides the material and the light; your rituals, how you wake, cook, receive and come to rest, provide the form.

Whether it is your home, an office, a shop or a place for guests: you work directly with one interior architect. The result feels personal, is timeless and is made to last.

Services

01Spatial advice

During a home visit of one to three hours, you and I get to know each other. I listen to how you live and what you want, and advise on site on what could change: in space, colour and style. If it feels right, you continue with Design or Design with guidance.

Discuss →
02Design

Based on the survey I develop a complete concept: plans of the existing and new situation, sections, 3D visualisations, a material and colour plan and a lighting plan. Two rounds of revision are included.

Discuss →
03Design with guidance

Everything from Design, plus a bill of quantities and the tender: I compare the quotes and follow up the site through to completion, in close cooperation with an experienced contractor or site manager.
