Services

01Spatial advice

During a two-to-three-hour home visit I measure your space and see how your household lives in it, sketching on-site. Within a week you receive a short report with two scenarios, hand sketches, a construction cost indication and advice on the tradespeople you will need. If you proceed to Design, this amount is fully deducted.

02Design

Based on the survey I develop a complete concept: plans of the existing and new situation, sections, 3D visualisations, a material and colour plan, a lighting plan and a bill of quantities. Two revision rounds are included.

03Design with guidance

Everything from Design, plus the tender: quotes are compared and the site is followed up through to completion, in close cooperation with an experienced contractor or site manager.
